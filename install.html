Install · Beta 0.1 · macOS

Install Mobi in two minutes.

Five steps from download to your first hint. You only do the security step once.

Download for Mac · 8 MB Apple Silicon & Intel · Free during beta · Ages 13+

  1. Download

    Click Download for Mac above. The file Mobi-mac.dmg lands in your Downloads folder.

  2. Drag it into Applications

    Open Mobi-mac.dmg, then drag the Mobi icon onto the Applications folder.

  3. Open it the first time

    Open Mobi from Applications. Your Mac will say it can't verify the app. Click Done, not Move to Trash.

    Then open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down, click Open Anyway next to Mobi, and enter your Mac password.

    This only happens once. Mobi is a new beta app that isn't registered with Apple yet.

  4. Create your account

    Enter your name and email, and confirm you're 13 or older. It's free during the beta.

  5. Allow Screen Recording

    Click Allow, switch Mobi on in System Settings, and choose Quit & Reopen if asked. Mobi only looks at your screen when you press a shortcut.

    Optional: allow Accessibility too, so Mobi can pause a lecture video when you capture it.

Shortcuts

⌥ SpaceShow or hide Mobi
⌥ ⇧ CCapture a problem
⌥ ⇧ LCapture a lecture frame
⌥ ⇧ HNext hint

⌥ is the Option key, ⇧ is Shift.

If something's off

Screen Recording keeps asking: in Privacy & Security → Screen Recording, switch Mobi off and on again, then reopen Mobi.

A shortcut does nothing: click the Mobi icon in the menu bar or Dock once, then try again.

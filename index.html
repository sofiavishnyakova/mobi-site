01

Three steps between you and unstuck.

⌥ ⇧ C

Capture the problem

Drag a box around any question on your screen: a worksheet, an online quiz, a textbook PDF. No typing it in, no copy-pasting.

⌥ ⇧ H

Get a hint, not the answer

Three levels of help, from "where do I even start?" to the next concrete step. You do the solving, which is how it actually sticks.

Check my work

Find the exact slip

Show it your attempt and it points to the line where things went wrong, then asks the question that helps you see why.

02

A tutor that looks over your shoulder.

Math you can trust

Every step it checks is verified by a symbolic math engine, not guessed by AI. If it can't prove a step is wrong, it won't tell you it is.

Play with the math

When a picture helps, you get an interactive graph. Drag a point along a curve, slide in more rectangles under it, and watch the idea click.

Pause a lecture, ask why

Capture a frame from any lecture video and click a line of the derivation to see why it follows from the one before.

Remembers what you studied

Every session is saved on your Mac. Reopen any problem, see the topics you stopped on, and export a study sheet before the test.

04 · Privacy

Your screen stays yours.

  • It only looks when you press the shortcut. Nothing runs in secret.
  • Screenshots are read once and thrown away, never stored.
  • Your study history lives on your own Mac.
05 · Install

Up and running in a minute.

  1. Download the file and open it.
  2. Drag Study Tutor into your Applications folder.
  3. Open it once. Because this is an early beta, macOS will say it can't verify the app. Click Done.
  4. Go to System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down, and click Open Anyway. You only do this once.
  5. The first time you capture, allow Screen Recording, then reopen the app. You're set.
